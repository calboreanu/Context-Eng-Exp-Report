# Data-availability language

## While the repository remains private

> Analysis code, rule contracts, aggregate outputs, data definitions, claim-to-evidence mappings, and cryptographic receipts have been assembled as a disclosure-review candidate. They are not yet publicly deposited. Raw and row-level workstation records are not approved for distribution because they can contain prompts, responses, tool traces, exact timestamps, paths, and confidential material.

## After an immutable public release exists

> Analysis code, rule contracts, aggregate outputs, a data dictionary, claim-to-evidence mappings, and integrity receipts are available at [immutable release URL and tag]. The release supports independent checking of the published aggregate arithmetic and traceability mappings. Raw prompts, responses, exact timestamps, paths, conversation identifiers, balanced row derivatives, and candidate-linkage records are not distributed because they may contain confidential information or permit trajectory reconstruction. Authorized source-level verification may be considered under an appropriate controlled-access agreement.

Do not replace the bracketed locator with a moving default-branch URL. Record the release tag, archive digest, and access date in the final manuscript source.
