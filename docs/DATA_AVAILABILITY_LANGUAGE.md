# Data-availability language

## While the repository remains private

> Analysis code, rule contracts, disclosure-approved aggregate outputs, data definitions, claim-to-evidence mappings, and cryptographic receipts have been assembled in a private release candidate. They are not yet publicly deposited. Raw and row-level workstation records are not approved for distribution because they can contain prompts, responses, tool traces, exact timestamps, paths, and confidential material.

## After an immutable public release exists

> Analysis code, rule contracts, aggregate outputs, a data dictionary, claim-to-evidence mappings, and integrity receipts are available at [immutable release URL and tag]. Code and executable specifications are licensed under Apache-2.0; documentation and public aggregate evidence are licensed under CC BY 4.0. The release supports independent checking of the published aggregate arithmetic and traceability mappings. Raw prompts, responses, exact timestamps, paths, conversation identifiers, balanced row derivatives, and candidate-linkage records are not distributed because they may contain confidential information or permit trajectory reconstruction. Authorized source-level verification may be considered under an appropriate controlled-access agreement.

Do not replace the bracketed locator with a moving default-branch URL. Record the release tag, archive digest, and access date in the final manuscript source.
