arithmetic, exact claim selectors, the disclosure boundary, manifested bytes and
fictional regression examples. Selector resolution locates evidence; it does not
compare every printed manuscript value or establish confidential-source validity.

verification for all claim families.

records and private receipts. Their scope is distinct from publicly executable
aggregate checks. Prompts, responses, supplied context, row-level derivatives,
exact timestamps, locators, identity maps, detailed human-review materials and
